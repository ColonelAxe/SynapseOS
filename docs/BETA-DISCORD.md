# Discord announcement — ready to copy

**ATLAS / SynapseOS — Closed Beta sign-ups**

We're preparing a small **4–8 player closed beta** for ATLAS, our Arma 3 tactical system inside SynapseOS.

**What we'll be testing**
• Tablet, phone, moving GPS and vehicle/aircraft interfaces
• Messaging, private channels and access permissions
• NATO contact reports, shared plans/routes and order tracking
• MEDEVAC/support requests, air coordination and TOC briefing screens
• UAV/helmet feeds and optional report-photo sharing
• Reconnects, joining mid-mission and compatibility with our modpack

The current core build passed **681 automated native checks**, plus **20 isolated dedicated-backend checks**. This beta is about finding the multiplayer and real-session issues those checks can't prove away. Expect bugs and a structured test session rather than a normal operation.

We're looking for command, ground, aviation and observer testers who can follow a short checklist and report reproducible problems. We'll start with the frozen CB1 build, CBA and original cTab; the organiser will publish the exact preset, download and session details separately. Please don't mix older SICS/ATLAS builds or cTab forks into the test.

**Interested? Reply with your preferred role and availability.**

Camera uses a Windows x64 native extension and may be unavailable under a tester's normal security policy; the rest of the beta can still be tested. No security settings need to be weakened.
